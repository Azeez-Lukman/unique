# TECHNICAL & STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

**Document purpose:** Record important project decisions, recommendations, outstanding questions and technical considerations.

## 1. Business positioning

### Recommended positioning

Unique Property & Construction Company should be presented as a construction and real estate services company.

Its two primary divisions should remain clearly distinguishable:

**Building & Construction**
- Residential construction.
- Commercial construction.

**Real Estate**
- Property management.
- Property sales.
- Property rentals.

### Proposed brand statement

"Building Dreams. Managing Properties. Creating Value."

Existing company tagline:

"If you can dream it, we can build it."

Recommendation: Retain the original tagline unless the business owner approves a change.

## 2. Website direction

### Recommended website type

A professional multi-page corporate website with conversion-focused service pages.

Avoid turning the website into a complex property marketplace at the initial stage.

### Recommended navigation

- **Home** (`/`) — Comprehensive company overview, dual-division showcase, process, and primary conversion point.
- **About Us** (`/about/`) — Brand values, commitment to quality, vision execution, operating standards.
- **Construction Services** (`/construction/`) — Residential builds, commercial construction, consultation scheduling.
- **Real Estate Services** (`/real-estate/`) — Property management, leasing facilitation, landlord peace of mind.
- **Properties** (`/properties/`) — Active sales and rental showcase; incorporates a client sourcing request mechanism.
- **Contact Us** (`/contact/`) — Interactive multi-category enquiry form, direct phone, WhatsApp channel.

*Architecture note:* Keeping Construction and Real Estate as separate dedicated service pages ensures targeted SEO and distinct conversion funnels for builders vs. property managers. Combining Sales and Rentals under a unified "Properties" section prevents fragmented navigation.

## 3. Visual identity

### Confirmed visual direction & logo integration

The official company logo establishes a prestigious, heritage architectural tone:
- **Primary Mark:** Circular seal containing a multi-gable architectural roof silhouette with speed/foundation lines.
- **Brand Typography:** Stately serif wordmark for **UNIQUE**, paired with clean, modern sans-serif for **Property and Construction**.
- **Confirmed Brand Palette:**

| Colour | Hex Code | Role | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Deep Forest Green** | `#1C3D28` | Primary Brand | Extracted directly from official logo. Evokes permanence, land, stability, and prestige. |
| **Spruce Dark** | `#112619` | Dark Neutral | Deep headers, footers, and high-contrast text surfaces. |
| **Warm Gold / Brass** | `#C5A059` | Primary Accent | Restrained accent for badges, key action CTAs, borders, and highlights. |
| **Warm Cream / Sand** | `#F4EFE6` | Surface Neutral | Soft secondary card backgrounds, subtle dividers, and feature highlights. |
| **Crisp Ivory** | `#FBFBF9` | Base Background | Primary page background ensuring high readability and clean contrast. |
| **Forest Slate** | `#526359` | Muted Text | Secondary typography, captions, and metadata. |

Typography pairing:
- **Display / Brand Accents:** `Playfair Display` (mirroring the classic serif letterforms of the logo's "UNIQUE").
- **Headings & Navigation:** `Outfit` (clean geometric clarity for architectural headers).
- **Body & Forms:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (highly readable, professional sans-serif).

## 4. Photography strategy

The business currently has no photographs or videos of completed projects.

Recommended approach:

1. Use properly licensed architectural stock photography for illustrative sections.
2. Clearly distinguish illustrative images from actual company projects.
3. Do not create fake project case studies.
4. Use genuine business photographs where available.
5. Create a dedicated space for future project documentation.
6. Encourage the owner to begin collecting photographs and videos from future work.

Do not describe stock images as the company's construction achievements.

## 5. Conversion strategy

The website should encourage visitors to make relevant enquiries.

Recommended enquiry categories:

- Construction project.
- Residential construction.
- Commercial construction.
- Property management.
- Property purchase.
- Property rental.
- General business enquiry.

A unified enquiry form with a category selector is recommended initially.

WhatsApp should be prominently accessible, alongside telephone and email contact options.

The final stack must be selected after inspecting the existing project and confirming hosting requirements.
