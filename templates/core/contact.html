Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a cost estimate for my construction project?

Submit your preliminary specifications, site location, or architectural drawings via our enquiry form. We conduct a preliminary feasibility review and schedule a consultation to prepare a detailed, itemized estimate.

What does your full-service property management include?

We handle everything from tenant sourcing, identity and income vetting, and legal lease preparation to monthly rent collection, emergency and routine maintenance, and scheduled property condition inspections.

Do you build both residential homes and commercial developments?

Yes. We operate dedicated teams experienced in bespoke single-family residential villas as well as commercial retail and office construction, ensuring relevant engineering standards and building codes are strictly adhered to.

Can you help me source land or a property to purchase?

Yes. Through our property sales and sourcing desk, clients provide us with target locations, zoning needs, and investment budgets, and we assist in identifying verified land plots or completed properties.
